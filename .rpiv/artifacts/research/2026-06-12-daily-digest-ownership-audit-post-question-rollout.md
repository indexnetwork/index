## Context: how questions got here today

1. Prod `questions` table was empty — `QUESTIONER_ENABLED` was unset (no enqueue
   injection, no worker) and `ENABLE_DISCOVERY_QUESTIONS=false`. Both flags now set.
2. Background queues (enrichment, profile-run, discovery-run) were constructed
   without `questionerEnqueue` — fixed in #943 via `questionerEnqueueIfEnabled()`.
3. Backfills executed against prod: profile-gap (4 users → 7 questions) and
   intent-refinement (102 users → 138 questions; one job per user, most recent
   active intent). Current: 146 pending questions across 103 users.
4. Digests archived + regenerated by operator; 92/95 eligible cards picked up a
   question (97% attach rate).
