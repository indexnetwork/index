### S1 🟡 `scrape_url` is exposed through generic pass-through wrappers

**Where**
`packages/hermes-plugin/tools.py:76`

**Code**
```python
        "scrape_url",
```

**Why**
The new Hermes surface registers `index_scrape_url`, and `packages/hermes-plugin/tools.py:367` forwards caller arguments unchanged to MCP. The protocol-side tool accepts a caller-supplied URL at `packages/protocol/src/shared/agent/utility.tools.ts:24` and passes the normalized URL into extraction at `packages/protocol/src/shared/agent/utility.tools.ts:33`, so this commit expands Hermes access to a user-controlled outbound fetch capability.

**Fix**
Either remove `scrape_url` from the forwarded Hermes allowlist or add/verify explicit URL-host/protocol restrictions at the backend scraper boundary before exposing it through Hermes.
