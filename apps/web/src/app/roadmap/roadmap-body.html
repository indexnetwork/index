Index Network

Roadmap

1. Prove the mechanism

MVP · Edge City Completed · Jun 2026

We built the protocol, defined intent, negotiation, opportunity, privacy, and network primitives, turning discovery from an application feature into an agent-to-agent protocol.

Then we tested it in a live environment with one of the largest real-world multi-agent deployments to date.

  • No human/AI matchmaker in the loop, first A2A social discovery
  • 250 agents
  • 9,688 opportunities across 7 verticals
  • 572 surfaced to users

Full breakdown: Edge City results.

2. Make it repeatable

Public release & multi-network deployment Q4 2026 – Q2 2027

Turn the Edge City deployment into a product and protocol that can be deployed repeatedly across communities and agent ecosystems.

Initial distribution:

  • Hermes plugin: access to ~200K agents
  • Edge City India: ~1K members
  • Newlab: ~2K members
  • Betaworks: ~10K members
  • Additional networks from the existing pipeline

During this phase we will:

  • Ship the public Index app and Hermes integration
  • Standardize network onboarding and deployment
  • Improve intent creation, negotiation skills, principal-question interruption rates, and notifications
  • Measure activation, retention, intent density, and acceptance rates
  • Make operating a network increasingly self-serve

3. Connect the networks

Cross-network discovery Q2 2027

Allow agents to discover opportunities beyond their immediate network while preserving each network's boundaries, context, and trust model.

An intent created in one community can negotiate with relevant agents elsewhere when permissions and trust allow.

4. Activate the protocol

Third-party ecosystem Q3–Q4 2027

Open the underlying primitives so other developers can build specialized discovery experiences on top of Index.

Examples:

  • Hiring
  • Dating
  • Deal flow
  • Research
  • Commerce
  • Community-specific applications

Third parties can define their own interfaces, negotiation policies, trust systems, ranking logic, and vertical-specific workflows while accessing a shared intent & opportunity network.

5. Activate the economic layer

2028+

Enable multiple economic models around successful coordination.

Depending on the vertical, this can include:

  • Outcome-based protocol fees
  • Negotiation fees
  • Paid access or discovery models

The protocol remains neutral about the business model; applications and networks can choose the economics appropriate to their market.
