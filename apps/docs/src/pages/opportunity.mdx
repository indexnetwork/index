# Opportunity [The record of one pair, from negotiation to decision]

An opportunity is the record of one pair. An agent creates it when it opens a
[negotiation](/negotiation) with a candidate that discovery returned. The
negotiation happens inside it. When the agents agree, the opportunity is put in
front of both people, with the reasoning for why it is worth each person's
time.

An opportunity is not a retrieval hit, and it is not a chat with n agents on one
log.

## Arity

An opportunity has exactly **two actors** and exactly **one negotiation**. The
turn grammar and the disclosure lattice are defined on those two seats.
Multi-party opportunities do not exist yet.

## What each actor can read

Both actors can read the opportunity as soon as it opens, while it is still
`negotiating`, and they keep that access in every later state, including
`rejected`. Discovery results that no agent opened never become opportunities.

The pair must share a [network](/network).

## One record, two projections

Each actor reads a card rather than the raw record:

- An explanation relative to their role — why this pair, why you, what you
  would do
- Their own next action

The other seat's private constraints never appear. When the presenter model
succeeds, each person gets their own paragraph. When it fails, both get the same
paragraph drawn from the opportunity's shared reasoning.

## Two clocks

An opportunity advances on two separate clocks:

1. **Agent-to-agent.** The agents settle the negotiation log. An accepted
   proposal moves the opportunity to `pending`; a decline moves it to
   `rejected`.
2. **Human.** Each actor decides for themselves. One seat cannot accept for
   another. Accepting a second time is refused; rejecting or expiring stays
   available to either actor.

**One accept opens the connection.** The first actor to accept makes the whole
opportunity `accepted` and opens a direct conversation between the two people.
Nothing waits for the other actor. A person's decision also ends a negotiation
that is still running.

Agent agreement is not enough. A later yes cannot override an earlier refusal.

## States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Negotiating | Opened by an agent; the negotiation is running inside it. |
| Pending | The agents agreed; in front of both people, awaiting a decision. |
| Accepted | One actor accepted; their conversation is open. |
| Rejected | An agent declined in the negotiation, or an actor rejected it. |
| Expired | One of its intents was archived. |

The API exposes these as `negotiating`, `pending`, `accepted`, `rejected`, and
`expired`.

Opportunities have no timeout today: `expired` comes only from archiving an
intent, and a negotiation that reaches the turn limit stays `negotiating`.

Accepting through `PATCH /api/opportunities/:id/status` or
`POST /api/opportunities/:id/start-chat`, which name no intent, also accepts
every other open opportunity between the same two people, in any network and
including ones still `negotiating`, and closes their negotiations. The
intent-scoped routes the web and Mac apps use accept only the one opportunity.

Declined, expired, and otherwise terminal opportunities do not advance again on
their own.
