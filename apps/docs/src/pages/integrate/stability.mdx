# Stability [What the contract covers]

`@indexnetwork/protocol` follows Semantic Versioning. The public contract is
exactly the set of symbols exported from the package root; deep imports may
change in any release.

Exports carry one of two tiers:

- **Stable** — interfaces, graph factories, agents, and shared schemas. A
  breaking change requires a major bump.
- **Experimental** — advanced graph state and internal helpers, marked
  `@experimental`. These may change in a minor release.

Breaking the public surface is allowed and expected at this stage: the major is
bumped, the change is recorded, and in-repo consumers are updated in the same
change.
